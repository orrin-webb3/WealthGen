Trading Laws

Law 1
Timeframe Discipline
tap to reveal
Law 1 — Timeframe
The timeframe you identify the setup on is the timeframe you manage the trade on. 3D setup is never managed like a day trade. 5M setup is never held overnight.
No exceptions.
Law 2
VRVP Placement
tap to reveal
Law 2 — VRVP
LVN = fast move highway. HVN = chop, wait for break. POC = strongest magnet. R:R must be 1.5 to nearest HVN target.
Can't answer all 3? No trade.
Law 3
TD Sequential
tap to reveal
Law 3 — TD Sequential
Count 1–4 = full size. Count 5 = half size. Count 6–8 = no entry. Count 9 = exhaustion only.
Never enter in direction of count 6, 7, 8, or 9.
Law 4
TraderXO Signal
tap to reveal
Law 4 — TraderXO
Bull + above 25 EMA = full conviction. Below 25 EMA = half size. From 20 band = highest quality. Near 80 band = do not enter.
Law 5
RSI Rules
tap to reveal
Law 5 — RSI
Below 40 = oversold, adds conviction. 40–65 = valid zone. Above 72 = hard veto. RSI diverging from price = exit longs immediately.
Law 6
Position Sizing
tap to reveal
Law 6 — Sizing
A-grade = full size. B-grade = half size. C-grade = paper only.
401K ceiling: $15,000 max cost basis. No exceptions.
Law 7
Stop Loss
tap to reveal
Law 7 — Stop
Set immediately at entry. Never widened. Only trail tighter. Position moves against thesis: hold to stop or exit — adding is never permitted.
Law 8
Daily Limits
tap to reveal
Law 8 — Limits
Max 2 trades/session on daily tier. Max 3 active positions across all tiers. 2 losses = done for the day.
No exceptions.
Law 9
Market Context
tap to reveal
Law 9 — Context
Never take a bullish trade on a stock when SPY is in a confirmed downtrend on the same timeframe as your setup.
Law 10
Journal Gate
tap to reveal
Law 10 — Journal
No journal entry, no trade. Answer: tier · signals · VRVP · entry/stop/target · R:R · invalidation.
The completed entry IS the win.
Law 11
TradingView Alerts
tap to reveal
Law 11 — Alerts
Alerts notify you to look, never to buy. Alert fires → chart → run laws → journal → all 6 questions → then consider entry.
Never trade the moment an alert fires on mobile.
Law 12
Account Rules
tap to reveal
Law 12 — Accounts
401K: stocks/ETFs only, regular hours, no averaging down, hold duration in journal. IBKR: options 30+ DTE A-grade, ETF options 15+ DTE B-grade.
Law 13
Execution Device
tap to reveal
Law 13 — Device
All executions on laptop only. No mobile. No iPad. Mobile trade ideas must be re-evaluated cold on laptop before any order.
Structural rule, not a preference.
Law 14
Chart Patterns
tap to reveal
Law 14 — Patterns
Identify candlestick patterns, chart patterns, and S/D zones on every chart. If no named pattern, say so explicitly.
Never leave pattern status ambiguous.
Law 15
Extended Hours

Law 15 — Ext. Hours
Never hold leveraged positions into earnings. 401K = regular hours only. IBKR = limit orders only extended hours.
Thin volume can gap past any stop.
